27. Question

Category: CSAA – Design Resilient Architectures

An online stock trading application stores financial data in an Amazon S3 bucket, with a lifecycle policy that moves older data to Glacier every month. A strict compliance requirement mandates that a surprise audit can occur at any time, and the required data must be retrievable in under 15 minutes under all circumstances. The manager has instructed that retrieval capacity be available when needed and should support up to 150 MB/s of retrieval throughput.

Which of the following will meet the given requirement? (Select TWO.)

- Specify a range, or portion, of the financial data archive to retrieve.
- Use Standard Retrieval for accessing the financial data.
- `Purchase provisioned retrieval capacity.`
- Use Bulk Retrieval to access the financial data.
- `Use Expedited Retrieval to access the financial data.`


Is question mein **Amazon S3 Glacier Flexible Retrieval Options** aur **Provisioned Capacity** ke exact parameters test ho rahe hain.

Aayein har requirement ko step-by-step break down karke dekhte hain ke kaun se **TWO** options sahi hain.

---

## 1. Core Requirements Breakdown

1. **Retrieval Time:** Data **under 15 minutes** mein retrievable hona chahiye (strict audit compliance).
2. **Guaranteed Capacity & Throughput:** Demand badhne par bhi retrieval access Guaranteed hona chahiye aur **150 MB/s throughput** deliver honi chahiye.

---

## 2. Core AWS Technical Rules for S3 Glacier

* **Expedited Retrieval:** Archives ko **1 to 5 minutes** (under 15 mins) mein restore karti hai.
* **Provisioned Capacity:** Guaranteed capacity deliver karti hai taake high demand peak times par bhi Expedited Requests refuse na hon. Har Provisioned Capacity Unit **at least 3 Expedited retrievals every 5 minutes** guarantee karti hai aur **150 MB/s throughput** tak provide karti hai.
* **Standard Retrieval:** **3 to 5 hours** leti hai.
* **Bulk Retrieval:** **5 to 12 hours** leti hai.

---

## 3. Correct Options Explanation (Select TWO)

#### ✅ **Use Expedited Retrieval to access the financial data.**

* **Why it works:** Expedited Retrieval hi akela aisa tier hai jo Glacier archives ko **1–5 minutes** mein restore kar deta hai, jo ke audit requirement (< 15 minutes) ko meet karta hai.

#### ✅ **Purchase provisioned retrieval capacity.**

* **Why it works:** Standard Expedited Requests "best effort" basis par chaltay hain. Question mein strict constraint hai ke *"retrieval capacity be available when needed and support up to 150 MB/s"*. **Provisioned Capacity** officially **150 MB/s per unit** ki retrieval throughput guarantee karti hai.

---

## 4. Incorrect Options Breakdown (Elimination Strategy)

| Option | Why It Fails the Exam Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Specify a range, or portion...** | S3 Byte-Range Fetches standard S3 objects par hote hain, lekin S3 Glacier restore time limit (under 15 minutes / 150 MB/s capacity guarantee) ko standalone solve nahi karte. |
| **Use Standard Retrieval...** | Standard Retrieval **3 to 5 hours** leti hai, jo 15-minute time window ki requirement ko satisfy nahi karti. |
| **Use Bulk Retrieval...** | Bulk Retrieval sab se slow hai (**5 to 12 hours**), is se compliance requirement violate ho jayegi. |

---

## 5. Exam Decision Matrix (S3 Glacier Retrieval Tiers Cheat Sheet)

* **1 to 5 Minutes (Urgent Access):** $\rightarrow$ **Expedited Retrieval**
* **Guaranteed 150 MB/s throughput for Expedited:** $\rightarrow$ **Provisioned Capacity**
* **3 to 5 Hours (Normal Access):** $\rightarrow$ **Standard Retrieval**
* **5 to 12 Hours (Lowest Cost / Large Batches):** $\rightarrow$ **Bulk Retrieval**

### 🎯 What is being tested?

**S3 Glacier retrieval speed + guaranteed retrieval capacity**

### ✅ Correct answers

1. **Purchase Provisioned Retrieval Capacity**
2. **Use Expedited Retrieval**

### 🔑 Key concept

* **Expedited Retrieval** → retrieves Glacier data in **1–5 minutes** → meets **under 15 minutes**.
* **Provisioned Retrieval Capacity** → guarantees that **Expedited Retrieval capacity is available when needed** and supports high retrieval throughput.

### ❌ Why others are wrong

* **Standard Retrieval** → hours ❌
* **Bulk Retrieval** → even slower ❌
* **Specify a range/portion** → only retrieves part of the object; doesn't guarantee retrieval speed ❌

### 🔥 Exam shortcut

**Glacier + <15 minutes → Expedited Retrieval**
**“Capacity guaranteed/available when needed” → Provisioned Retrieval Capacity**
