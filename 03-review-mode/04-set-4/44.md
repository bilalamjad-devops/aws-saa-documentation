44. Question

Category: CSAA – Design Secure Architectures

An online survey startup is collecting real estate data in the United States for several years. The startup already has a total of 5 TB of data stored in an Amazon S3 bucket located in the us-east-1 Region. All real estate data must be shared with a European AWS Managed Service Provider (MSP) Partner which also uses Amazon S3 for storage. Due to budget constraints, the startup must keep its data transfer costs in S3 as low as possible and disable anonymous access.

Which solution meets this requirement MOST cost-effectively?

- Enable cross-account access of the startup’s S3 bucket to allow the data downloads and exclusive access from the partner’s AWS account
- Enable Cross-Region Replication(CRR) on the startup’s S3 bucket to automatically copy the S3 content to the partner’s S3 bucket in Europe.
- `Enable the Requester Pays feature on the Amazon S3 bucket to lower data transfer costs and disable anonymous access`
- Enable S3 Object Lock in governance mode to lower data transfer costs and set a Legal Hold for each object to disable anonymous access

Is question mein **Amazon S3 Data Transfer Costs**, **Cross-Account Sharing**, aur **Requester Pays** ka concept test ho raha hai.

Aayein requirements aur options ko Roman Urdu mein Step-by-Step analyze karte hain:

---

### 1. Requirement Breakdown

1. **Source & Volume:** Startup ke paas 5 TB real estate data **`us-east-1` Region** ke S3 bucket mein pada hua hai.
2. **Sharing Goal:** Yeh data Europe mein mojood ek Partner (AWS MSP) ke sath share karna hai.
3. **Key Constraints:**
* Budget constraints ki wajah se **Data Transfer Out costs ko minimize/zero karna hai**.
* **Anonymous (Public) access completely disable** rakhna hai.


4. **Goal:** S3 ka sab se cost-effective mechanism chun-na.

---

### 2. AWS S3 Billing & Data Transfer Mechanics

Standard S3 setups mein:

* **Bucket Owner Pays:** Default tor par jab koi external user S3 bucket se data download karta hai, toh **Data Transfer Out (DTO) ka bill bucket owner (Startup) ko jata hai**. 5 TB data transfer karne ka bill kafi ziada ho sakta hai.
* **Requester Pays Feature:** Jab aap S3 bucket par **Requester Pays** enable kar dete hain:
* Download karne wala (Requester / European Partner) data transfer costs aur request charges khud pay karta hai.
* Startup (Bucket owner) ko 5 TB download ka $0 data transfer charge padta hai.
* Downloading ke liye Requester ko AWS credentials ke sath authenticate hona parta hai, jis se anonymous access automatically block/disable rehta hai.



---

### 3. Correct Option Explanation

#### ✅ **Enable the Requester Pays feature on the Amazon S3 bucket to lower data transfer costs and disable anonymous access**

* **Why it works:** Requester Pays data download ka bill data maangne wale (Partner) ke account par daal deta hai, jis se startup ki data transfer cost drop ho kar zero ho jati hai. Is feature ko use karne ke liye requester ka authenticated hona zaroori hai, jo public/anonymous access ko restrict karta hai.

---

### 4. Incorrect Options Breakdown (Elimination Strategy)

| Option | Why It Fails |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable cross-account access...** | Cross-account bucket policy se partner ko access toh mil jayega, lekin **default billing owner (Startup)** hi rahega. Data Transfer Out charges startup ko bil honge. |
| **Enable Cross-Region Replication (CRR)...** | CRR enable karne se **5 TB data replication charge + destination S3 storage charge** startup/owner ko pay karna parega, jo bohot expensive hai. |
| **Enable S3 Object Lock in governance mode...** | S3 Object Lock WORM (Write Once Read Many) compliance aur compliance/retention enforcement ke liye hota hai; iska data transfer costs kam karne se koi taluq nahi hai. |

---

### 5. Cheat Sheet for AWS S3 Cost Offloading (SAA-C03)

* **Offload Data Transfer Out costs to external consumers:** $\rightarrow$ **Enable Requester Pays on S3 Bucket**
* **Replicate data automatically to another region for low latency:** $\rightarrow$ **S3 Cross-Region Replication (CRR)**
* **Grant granular permissions without transferring ownership:** $\rightarrow$ **S3 Bucket Policies / Cross-Account IAM Roles**

### Simple Life Example

Maan lein aap ki ek software company hai. Aap apni IT maintenance ke liye ek bahar ki IT company ko contract de dete hain jo aap ke saare servers, networking, aur security ko manage karti hai.

AWS ki dunya mein isi bahar ki expert company ko **Managed Service Provider (MSP)** kehte hain.

---

### Breakdown (Lafz Ba Lafz Meaning)

1. **MSP (Managed Service Provider):**
Yeh aisi third-party IT companies hoti hain jo doosri companies ke AWS infrastructure ko setup, run, manage, aur secure karti hain.
2. **AWS Partner:**
Yeh ordinary companies nahi hoti. AWS inhe officially **Certify** karta hai ke yeh AWS cloud par kaam karne mein experts hain.
3. **European:**
Iska matlab hai ke yeh MSP partner company **Europe region** (maslan Germany, UK, ya France) mein located hai.

---

### Question Ke Context Mein Iska Kya Matlab Tha?

Question mein **Startup (US-based)** apna 5 TB real estate data Europe mein mojood ek **MSP Partner Company** ke sath share karna chahta tha.

Kyunki data download karne wali company **Europe mein thi**, is liye agar **Requester Pays** enable na kiya jata, toh US se Europe data bhejney ka poora **Data Transfer Out Bill (5 TB ka bill)** US wali startup ko bharna padta.
