51. Question

Category: CSAA – Design High-Performing Architectures

A company has a High Performance Computing (HPC) cluster that is composed of EC2 Instances with Provisioned IOPS (io1) volume to process transaction-intensive, low-latency workloads. The Solutions Architect must maintain high IOPS while keeping the latency down by setting the optimal queue length for the volume. The size of each volume is 10 GiB.

Which of the following is the MOST suitable configuration that the Architect should set up?

- Set the IOPS to 600 then maintain a high queue length.
- Set the IOPS to 400 then maintain a low queue length.
- `Set the IOPS to 500 then maintain a low queue length.`
- Set the IOPS to 800 then maintain a low queue length.

Is question mein **EBS Volume Types (Provisioned IOPS - io1)**, **Max IOPS-to-GiB Ratios**, aur **I/O Queue Lengths** ka storage performance concept test ho raha hai.

Aayein requirements aur options ko Roman Urdu mein Step-by-Step analyze karte hain:

---

### 1. Requirement Breakdown

1. **Volume Type:** Provisioned IOPS SSD (**`io1`**).
2. **Volume Size:** **10 GiB**.
3. **Goal:** Low latency aur high IOPS maintain karna.
4. **Question:** Optimal **IOPS** aur **Queue Length** setting kya honi chahiye?

---

### 2. Technical Evaluation & AWS EBS Rules

#### A. Provisioned IOPS (`io1`) Ratio Limits

AWS mein `io1` volumes ke liye **Maximum IOPS-to-GiB ratio = 50:1** hota hai.

* Real calculation:

$$\text{Max Provisioned IOPS} = \text{Volume Size (GiB)} \times 50$$


$$\text{Max Provisioned IOPS} = 10 \text{ GiB} \times 50 = 500 \text{ IOPS}$$



Iska matlab hai ke **10 GiB ke `io1` volume par maximum 500 IOPS hi provision kiye ja sakte hain**. 500 se ziada (jaise 600 ya 800) set karna technical limitation ki waja se allowed nahi hai.

#### B. Optimal Queue Length for Low Latency

* **Queue Length** batati hai ke kitni I/O requests disk ke process hone ka wait kar rahi hain.
* Low latency workloads (jaise High-Performance Computing / Transaction Intensive DBs) ke liye **low queue length maintain karna zaroori hai**. Higher queue length ka matlab hota hai ke requests line mein khari hain, jis se latency (delay) barhti hai.

---

### 3. Correct Option Explanation

#### ✅ **Set the IOPS to 500 then maintain a low queue length.**

* **Why it works:** 10 GiB volume size par maximum limit $10 \times 50 = 500 \text{ IOPS}$ hai. Latency ko minimum rakhne ke liye queue length ko low rakha jata hai.

---

### 4. Incorrect Options Breakdown (Elimination Strategy)

| Option | Why It Fails |
| --- | --- |
| **Set IOPS to 600...** | Maximum ratio rule (50:1) ko break karta hai ($10 \times 50 = 500 \text{ max}$). High queue length latency ko barha deti hai. |
| **Set IOPS to 400...** | Although 400 set karna allowed hai, lekin requirement "maintain high IOPS" ki hai. 10 GiB par max performance 500 IOPS se milti hai. |
| **Set IOPS to 800...** | 50:1 ratio limit violate hoti hai (10 GiB volume 800 IOPS support nahi karta). |

---

### 5. Cheat Sheet for EBS io1 / io2 Performance (SAA-C03)

* **`io1` Max IOPS-to-GiB Ratio:** $\rightarrow$ **50:1** (Up to 64,000 IOPS max per volume)
* **`io2` Max IOPS-to-GiB Ratio:** $\rightarrow$ **500:1**
* **Low Latency Workloads:** $\rightarrow$ **Keep Queue Length Low**
