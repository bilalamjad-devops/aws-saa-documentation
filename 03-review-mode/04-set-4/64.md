64. Question

Category: CSAA – Design Resilient Architectures

A company recently launched an e-commerce application that is running in eu-east-2 region, which strictly requires six EC2 instances running at all times. In that region, there are 3 Availability Zones (AZ) that you can use – eu-east-2a, eu-east-2b, and eu-east-2c.

Which of the following deployments provide 100% fault tolerance if any single AZ in the region becomes unavailable? (Select TWO.)

- eu-east-2a with four EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with two EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with two EC2 instances
- eu-east-2a with two EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with four EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with two EC2 instances
- `eu-east-2a with six EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with six EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with no EC2 instances`
- `eu-east-2a with three EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with three EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with three EC2 instances`
- eu-east-2a with two EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with two EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with two EC2 instances

Is question mein **High Availability (HA)**, **Fault Tolerance (FT) Math**, aur **Availability Zone (AZ) Failure Sizing** ka calculation concept test ho raha hai.

Aayein requirements aur options ko step-by-step calculate karte hain:

---

### 1. Requirement Breakdown

1. **Target:** E-commerce application ko har waqt **minimum 6 EC2 instances** chalte hue chahiye.
2. **Infrastructure:** Region mein **3 Availability Zones (AZs)** hain: `eu-east-2a`, `eu-east-2b`, `eu-east-2c`.
3. **Condition:** Agar **koi BHI EK single AZ poora fail (down)** ho jaye, toh baki bache hue AZs mein **kam se kam 6 instances active rehne chahiye** (100% Fault Tolerance).

---

### 2. Math & Scenario Testing

Aayein har option ko test karte hain agar sab se bada ya critical AZ down ho jaye:

#### Option 1: `2a = 4`, `2b = 2`, `2c = 2`

* Agar **`eu-east-2a` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2b (2)` + `2c (2)` = **4 instances** ❌ *(Requirements 6 thi, 4 reh gaye – Failed)*



#### Option 2: `2a = 2`, `2b = 4`, `2c = 2`

* Agar **`eu-east-2b` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2a (2)` + `2c (2)` = **4 instances** ❌ *(Requirements 6 thi, 4 reh gaye – Failed)*



#### Option 3: `2a = 6`, `2b = 6`, `2c = 0`

* Agar **`eu-east-2a` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2b (6)` + `2c (0)` = **6 instances** ✅ *(Minimum 6 target poora ho gaya)*


* Agar **`eu-east-2b` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2a (6)` + `2c (0)` = **6 instances** ✅ *(Minimum 6 target poora ho gaya)*


* Agar **`eu-east-2c` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2a (6)` + `2b (6)` = **12 instances** ✅ *(Minimum 6 target poora ho gaya)*



#### Option 4: `2a = 3`, `2b = 3`, `2c = 3`

* Agar **`eu-east-2a` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2b (3)` + `2c (3)` = **6 instances** ✅ *(Minimum 6 target poora ho gaya)*


* Agar **`eu-east-2b` ya `2c` fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = **6 instances** ✅ *(Minimum 6 target poora ho gaya)*



#### Option 5: `2a = 2`, `2b = 2`, `2c = 2`

* Normal capacity = 6. Lekin agar **1 AZ fail** ho gaya:
* Remaining = `2 + 2` = **4 instances** ❌ *(Requirements 6 thi, 4 reh gaye – Failed)*



---

### 3. Correct Options Explanation

#### ✅ **eu-east-2a with six EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with six EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with no EC2 instances**

* **Why it works:** Total 12 instances deployed hain (6 in AZ-A, 6 in AZ-B). Agar AZ-A ya AZ-B mein se koi ek complete fail ho jata hai, toh doosre AZ mein pehle se exact 6 active instances chal rahe hain.

#### ✅ **eu-east-2a with three EC2 instances, eu-east-2b with three EC2 instances, and eu-east-2c with three EC2 instances**

* **Why it works:** Total 9 instances spread over 3 AZs (3 per AZ). Agar koi bhi 1 AZ down hota hai, toh bache hue 2 AZs mil kar $3 + 3 = 6$ instances active rakhte hain.

---

### 4. Cheat Sheet for AWS Fault Tolerance Sizing (SAA-C03)

* **Formula for AZ Failure Sizing:**
$$\text{Target Capacity} \le \text{Total Capacity} - \text{Capacity of Largest AZ}$$


* **N+1 Rule / Spread Across 3 AZs:** Spread equal capacity across all available AZs ($N+1$) for cost-effective 100% fault tolerance.
