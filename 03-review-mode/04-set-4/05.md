5. Question

Category: CSAA – Design Cost-Optimized Architectures

A web application requires a minimum of six Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (EC2) instances running at all times. You are tasked to deploy the application to three availability zones in the EU Ireland region (eu-west-1a, eu-west-1b, and eu-west-1c). It is required that the system is fault-tolerant up to the loss of one Availability Zone.

Which of the following setup is the most cost-effective solution which also maintains the fault-tolerance of your system?

- 6 instances in eu-west-1a, 6 instances in eu-west-1b, and no instances in eu-west-1c
- `3 instances in eu-west-1a, 3 instances in eu-west-1b, and 3 instances in eu-west-1c`
- 6 instances in eu-west-1a, 6 instances in eu-west-1b, and 6 instances in eu-west-1c
- 2 instances in eu-west-1a, 2 instances in eu-west-1b, and 2 instances in eu-west-1c

Is question mein **High Availability (HA), Fault Tolerance**, aur **Cost Optimization** ka calculation test ho raha hai.

Aayein requirement ko simple step-by-step math aur scenario breakdown se samajhte hain:

---

## 1. Requirement Breakdown

1. **Minimum Fleet Size:** Application ko har waqt kam se kam **6 EC2 instances** chalte hue chahiye.
2. **Distribution:** Instances ko 3 Availability Zones (`eu-west-1a`, `eu-west-1b`, `eu-west-1c`) mein distribute karna hai.
3. **Fault Tolerance Constraint:** System **1 Availability Zone (AZ) ke completely down hone par bhi 6 instances** maintain kar sake.
4. **Goal:** Sub se **cost-effective** (sab se kam total instances) setup batayein jo target acheive kare.

---

## 2. Testing Each Option (AZ Outage Simulation)

Agar 1 AZ fail ho jaye, toh bache hue 2 AZs mein kam se kam 6 instances active hone chahiye:

| Option | Normal Capacity (AZ A / AZ B / AZ C) | Total Running Instances | Capacity After 1 AZ Fails (Worst-Case) | Meets 6 Instances Requirement? | Total Cost Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **3 / 3 / 3** | 3 in AZ-A, 3 in AZ-B, 3 in AZ-C | **9 instances** | **6 instances left** ($3 + 3$) | ✅ **YES** | 💰 **Most Cost-Effective** |
| **2 / 2 / 2** | 2 in AZ-A, 2 in AZ-B, 2 in AZ-C | **6 instances** | **4 instances left** ($2 + 2$) | ❌ **NO** (Capacity drops below 6) | Under-provisioned |
| **6 / 6 / 0** | 6 in AZ-A, 6 in AZ-B, 0 in AZ-C | **12 instances** | **6 instances left** ($6 + 0$) | ✅ YES | ❌ Over-provisioned (12 > 9) |
| **6 / 6 / 6** | 6 in AZ-A, 6 in AZ-B, 6 in AZ-C | **18 instances** | **12 instances left** ($6 + 6$) | ✅ YES | ❌ Extremely Expensive |

---

## 3. Correct Option Explanation

#### ✅ **3 instances in eu-west-1a, 3 instances in eu-west-1b, and 3 instances in eu-west-1c**

* **Why it works:** Total 9 instances run honge. Agar koi ek AZ mukammal taur par down ho jati hai, toh baqi 2 AZs ke andar $3 + 3 = 6$ instances active rahenge, jisse application ka minimum threshold bilkul meet ho jayega with the lowest possible cost (9 instances total).

---

## 4. Exam Decision Matrix (Fault Tolerance Math Cheat Sheet)

* **Formula for Minimum Cost with AZ Fault Tolerance:**

$$\text{Instances per AZ} = \frac{\text{Required Minimum Instances}}{\text{Total AZs} - \text{Failed AZs}}$$


$$\text{Instances per AZ} = \frac{6}{3 - 1} = 3 \text{ instances per AZ}$$
