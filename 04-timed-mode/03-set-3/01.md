1. Question


An online stock trading application stores financial data in an Amazon S3 bucket, with a lifecycle policy that moves older data to Glacier every month. A strict compliance requirement mandates that a surprise audit can occur at any time, and the required data must be retrievable in under 15 minutes under all circumstances. The manager has instructed that retrieval capacity be available when needed and should support up to 150 MB/s of retrieval throughput.

Which of the following will meet the given requirement? (Select TWO.)

- Specify a range, or portion, of the financial data archive to retrieve.
- Use Standard Retrieval for accessing the financial data.
- `Purchase provisioned retrieval capacity.`
- `Use Expedited Retrieval to access the financial data.`
- Use Bulk Retrieval to access the financial data.

### Keywords Scan 🔍

1. **"retrievable in under 15 minutes under all circumstances"**
* **Trigger:** S3 Glacier retrieval speed under 15 minutes -> **Expedited Retrieval** (takes 1–5 minutes).


2. **"retrieval capacity be available when needed and should support up to 150 MB/s"**
* **Trigger:** Guaranteed retrieval capacity for Expedited retrievals during surprise audits -> **Provisioned Capacity**.



---

### Correct Answers (Select TWO)

* **Use Expedited Retrieval to access the financial data.**
* **Purchase provisioned retrieval capacity.**

---

### Question Aur Answer Ka Breakdown 💡

#### Question Kya Keh Raha Hai? ❓

* Ek stock trading application ka financial data Amazon S3 se **S3 Glacier Flexible Retrieval** mein lifecycle rule ke zariye move ho raha hai.
* Compliance requirement hai ke **surprise audit (baghair bataye inspection)** kabhi bhi ho sakti hai aur data **15 minutes se kam waqt mein retrieve** hona lazmi hai.
* Manager ne yeh bhi kaha hai ke retrieval throughput **150 MB/s** tak support honi chahiye aur jab data chahiye ho, capacity guaranteed available ho.

#### Answer Kya Keh Raha Hai? 💡

* **Expedited Retrieval:** S3 Glacier mein 3 kisam ki retrieval options hoti hain:
1. *Bulk Retrieval:* 5–12 hours lagte hain (sab se sasta).
2. *Standard Retrieval:* 3–5 hours lagte hain.
3. *Expedited Retrieval:* **1–5 minutes** mein data mil jata hai (jo 15 minutes ki requirement ko easily meet karta hai).


* **Provisioned Capacity:** Expedited retrievals normally best-effort basis par hoti hain (yani agar S3 par heavy load ho toh delay aa sakta hai). Lekin jab aap **Provisioned Capacity** purchase kar lete hain, toh AWS guarantee deta hai ke aap ki Expedited retrieval capacity har waqt available rahegi aur high throughput (up to 150 MB/s) support karegi.

---

### Baki Options Ghalt Kyun Hain? ❌

* **Specify a range, or portion, of the financial data archive to retrieve:** Range retrieval se transfer size kam ho sakta hai, lekin yeh 15-minute SLA aur guaranteed 150 MB/s throughput ki requirement ko akela resolve nahi karta.
* **Standard Retrieval:** Isme 3 se 5 ghante (hours) lagte hain, jo 15 minutes ki limit se boht zyada hai.
* **Bulk Retrieval:** Isme 5 se 12 ghante (hours) lagte hain.

### Glacier Retrieval Options Cheat Sheet ⚡

| Retrieval Type | Retrieval Time | Main Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Expedited** | **1–5 minutes** | Emergency / Urgent audits (Urgent access needed in < 15 mins) |
| **Standard** | **3–5 hours** | Normal operational queries |
| **Bulk** | **5–12 hours** | Large batch data processes (Lowest cost) |

**Expedited** (ya verbs: *expedite*) ka lafzi matlab hota hai: **"Tez tar karna"**, **"Jaldi niptana"**, ya **"Foori taur par rawana karna"**.

---

### Lafzi Maani & Real-Life Usages 📝

* **Expedite (Verb):** Kisi kaam ki raftaar ko barhana taake wo time se pehle ya foori taur par ho jaye (e.g., *"Please expedite my passport processing."* — *"Meharbani karke mera passport jaldi banwa dein."*).
* **Expedited (Adjective):** Ek aisa process jo normal se zyada **Fast-Track / Urgent** ho.

---

### Real-World Analogy 🚚✈️

| Normal Process (Standard) | Expedited Process (Tez/Urgent) |
| --- | --- |
| Normal Courier/Postage (Jo 5 se 7 din mein parcel pahunchata hai). | **Urgent / Urgent Express Delivery** (Jo extra charges le kar 24 ghante mein delivery deta hai). |

---

### AWS S3 Glacier Context ☁️⚡

AWS S3 Glacier mein **"Expedited Retrieval"** ka matlab hi yahi hai ke yeh aap ki data nikalne ki request ko **Fast-Track (Urgent)** lane mein daal deta hai, taake 3-5 ghante (Standard Time) ke bajaye sirf **1 se 5 minutes** mein data mil jaye!
