37. Question


A Solutions Architect working for a startup is designing a High Performance Computing (HPC) application which is publicly accessible for their customers. The startup founders want to mitigate distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks on their application.

Which of the following options are not suitable to be implemented in this scenario? (Select TWO.)

- `Use Dedicated EC2 instances to ensure that each instance has the maximum performance possible.`
- Use an Amazon CloudFront service for distributing both static and dynamic content.
- Use AWS Shield and AWS WAF.
- Use an Application Load Balancer with Auto Scaling groups for your EC2 instances. Prevent direct Internet traffic to your Amazon RDS database by deploying it to a new private subnet.
- `Add multiple Elastic Fabric Adapters (EFA) to each EC2 instance to increase the network bandwidth.`

Koi baat nahi! Is sawal mein thori si confusion yeh hoti hai ke yeh ek **"NOT suitable" (yaani konsi cheezein DDoS attack rokne ke liye ghalt/beasar hain)** wala sawal tha.

Aayein isay ek boht aasan real-life misaal se samajhte hain! 💡

---

### Real-Life Analogy: Dukan par Hamla 🏪🔨

Socho aap ki ek bari online store ki dukan hai, aur achanak **hazaron fake log (DDoS Attack)** aakar aap ki dukan ka darwaza peetne lagte hain taake dukan crash ho jaye.

Ab dekhte hain ke baqi options aur yeh do ghalt options kaisay kaam karti hain:

1. **Dedicated EC2 Instances (Kyun ghalt hai?):**
* *Misaal:* Iska matlab hai ke aap ne jo dukan rent par li hai, us building ka wo kamra **sirf aur sirf aap ka hai**, wahan kisi aur ki dukan nahi hai (Single-Tenant physical hardware).
* *Asar:* Lekin agar bahar kharay log aap ki dukan par patthar phenk rahe hain, toh kya is se farq parta hai ke aap ka kamra akele ka hai ya share wala? **Bilkul nahi!** Hacker phir bhi internet ke zariye attack kar sakta hai. Isliye yeh DDoS rokne ke liye beasar hai.


2. **Elastic Fabric Adapter - EFA (Kyun ghalt hai?):**
* *Misaal:* EFA ka matlab hai ke aap ne dukan ke **andar** ek super-fast **Ferrari racing car** rakhi hui hai jo dukan ke ek kone se dusre kone tak samaan palak jhapakte pohncha deti hai (Internal High-Speed HPC networking).
* *Asar:* Bhai, woh Ferrari toh dukan ke **andar** chal rahi hai! Bahar jo internet par hazaron log hamla kar rahe hain, unhein wo Ferrari kaisay rokay gi? EFA ka internet attacks se door door tak koi taalluq nahi hai.



---

### Asal Hal Kya Hai? (DDoS Rokne ke liye) 🛡️

DDoS attack rokne ke liye humein aisi cheezein chahiye jo internet ke traffic ko filter karein, jaise **CloudFront, AWS Shield, aur WAF**, jo aane wale gande traffic ko raste hi mein block kar dete hain.

Isliye question ne pucha tha ke **konsi 2 cheezein DDoS ke liye bekaar (not suitable) hain**, toh hum ne **Dedicated Instances** aur **EFA** select kiya!
