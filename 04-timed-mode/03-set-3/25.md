25. Question


A company is setting up a cloud architecture for an international money transfer service to be deployed in AWS which will have thousands of users around the globe. The service should be available 24/7 to avoid any business disruption and should be resilient enough to handle the outage of an entire AWS region. To meet this requirement, the Solutions Architect has deployed their AWS resources to multiple AWS Regions. He needs to use Route 53 and configure it to set all of the resources to be available all the time as much as possible. When a resource becomes unavailable, Route 53 should detect that it’s unhealthy and stop including it when responding to queries.

Which of the following is the most fault-tolerant routing configuration that the Solutions Architect should use in this scenario?

- Configure an Active-Passive Failover with Weighted Records.
- `Configure an Active-Active Failover with Weighted routing policy.`
- Configure an Active-Active Failover with One Primary and One Secondary Resource.
- Configure an Active-Passive Failover with Multiple Primary and Secondary Resources.


Koi baat nahi! Aayein **Active-Active** aur **Active-Passive** ko ek dum simple real-life misaal se samajhte hain, phir aap ko foran samajh aa jayega! 🏢💡

---

### 1. Real-Life Analogy: Restaurant ke Cash Counters 🍔

Socho aap ka ek bara restaurant hai jahan hazaron log aate hain:

* **Active-Active (Dono Counters Khule Hain):**
* Aap ne **Counter A** aur **Counter B** dono par cashier bitha diye hain.
* Aadhe log Counter A par ja rahe hain, aadhe Counter B par. Dono parallel kaam kar rahe hain.
* Agar achanak **Counter B kharab ho jaye**, toh sara customer foran Counter A par chala jata hai. Isay kehte hain **Active-Active** (Dono har waqt zinda/active hain aur kaam kar rahe hain).


* **Active-Passive (Aik Chalu, Doosra Soya Hua / Backup):**
* Aap ne **Counter A** par main cashier bitha diya jo saara kaam kar raha hai (**Active**).
* **Counter B** par aap ne aik dusra banda bitha kar bol diya: *"Bhai tum chup chap aram karo, jab tak Counter A theek hai tumne kuch nahi karna. Jab Counter A fail hoga, tab tumne aana hai"* (**Passive / Standby**).



---

### 2. Question Ke Context Mein Farq ☁️

* **Question kya chahta tha?**
* Question mein likha tha ke company chahti hai ke **"all of the resources to be available all the time as much as possible"** (yaani koi bhi resource fuzool ya standby baith kar apna paisa zaya na kare, **sare active hon**).


* Isliye hum ne **Active-Active** configuration choose ki, jisme Weighted routing policy ke zariye multiple regions active rehte hain aur traffic share karte hain.

---

### Comparison Table 📊

| Feature | Active-Active | Active-Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kaam karne ka tareeqa** | Saare servers aik sath live traffic handle karte hain. | Aik server chal raha hota hai, dusra sirf backup ke liye baitha hota hai. |
| **Resource Usage** | 100% utilization (Paisa zaya nahi hota). | Backup server idle rehta hai jab tak failover na ho. |
| **Best For** | High availability & global scaling. | Disaster Recovery (DR) backups. |
