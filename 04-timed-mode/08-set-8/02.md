2. Question

A startup wants to improve service availability and fault tolerance by distributing incoming traffic across multiple servers. The company uses Auto Scaling Group (ASG) of Amazon EC2 instances in the us-east-1 region. They plan to deploy the same setup in the us-west-2 region.

The company wants to automatically redirect traffic away from unhealthy instances, irrespective of their region.

Which of the following policies fulfills the company’s needs?

- Use Amazon Route 53's geoproximity routing policy to distribute incoming traffic to the other region.
- `Configure Route 53 and opt for a multivalue answer routing policy.`
- Launch an Application Load Balancer within the us-east-1 region. Configure the target group to include the IP addresses of Amazon EC2 instances across the two regions.
- Set up an Application Load Balancer in the us-east-1 region and configure it with a target group containing EC2 instances from both regions, identified by their instance IDs.

---

### 🧠 Question kya keh raha hai? (Scenario Breakdown)

1. **Multi-Region Setup:** Ek company ke paas `us-east-1` region mein ek Auto Scaling Group (ASG) hai, aur ab woh aisi hi same setup `us-west-2` (doosri region) mein bhi deploy karna chahte hain.
2. **Main Requirement:** Company chahti hai ke incoming traffic ko regions ke darmiyan distribute kiya jaye, aur agar koi bhi server/instance **unhealthy** ho jaye, toh traffic automatically uss unhealthy instance se door (redirect) ho jaye—chahe woh kisi bhi region mein ho.
3. **Options ka tajziya (Analysis):**
* **ALB Options (Options 3 & 4):** Application Load Balancer (ALB) ek **Regional service** hai. Ek region ka ALB (maslan `us-east-1` ka ALB) doosri region (`us-west-2`) ke EC2 instances ya IPs ko direct apne target group mein add **nahi** kar sakta. Is liye ALB wale dono options **galat** hain.
* **Route 53 Options (Options 1 & 2):** Amazon Route 53 ek global DNS service hai jo cross-region traffic management aur health checks ke liye sab se behtareen hai.



---

### ✅ Sahi Option Aur Uski Wajah:

Iska sahi jawab **Amazon Route 53 ki routing policies aur health checks** ka istemal hai.

* **Multivalue Answer Routing Policy (Option 2):** Route 53 ki yeh policy aapko har resource ke liye multiple IP addresses (ya endpoints) configure karne ki ijazat deti hai aur Route 53 Health Checks ke zariye sirf **healthy** targets ko hi return karti hai. Jab koi instance unhealthy hota hai, Route 53 health check usay detect karke traffic bhejna band kar deta hai, jo ke is scenario ki requirement ko poora karta hai.
* (Similarly, **Geoproximity routing policy** geographic location aur traffic bias ke liye use hoti hai, lekin multi-region availability aur health check ke sath traffic routing ke liye Multivalue Answer ya Failover/Latency policies effective hoti hain).
