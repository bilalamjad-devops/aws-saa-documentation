2. Question

A retail company stores customer transaction data on AWS. The data sees high access rates during seasonal sales and fluctuating access patterns throughout the rest of the year. The company is looking for a cost-effective storage solution that guarantees both durability and high availability to accommodate these varying access patterns.

Which Amazon S3 storage classes fulfill these requirements?

- Amazon S3 Express One Zone
- Amazon S3 Standard-Infrequent Access
- Amazon S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval
- `Amazon S3 Intelligent-Tiering`

### 1. Sawal Kya Keh Raha Hai? (Question Breakdown) ❓

* **Scenario:** Aik retail company customer transaction data AWS par store karti hai. Is data par **seasonal sales ke doran high access rates** hote hain, aur saal ke baqi arsay mein access patterns **fluctuate (badalte)** rehte hain (kabhi zyada, kabhi kam).
* **Requirements:**
1. Cost-effective storage solution chahiye.
2. **Durability aur High Availability** ki guarantee honi chahiye.
3. **Varying / Unpredictable access patterns** ko automatically handle kar sake (bina manual intervention ke).



---

### 2. Sahi Jawab Kya Keh Raha Hai? (Correct Answer) 💡

* **Option:** *Amazon S3 Intelligent-Tiering*
* **Wazahat:**
* **S3 Intelligent-Tiering:** Yeh storage class khas tor par un data ke liye bani hai jinke **access patterns unknown, changing, ya unpredictable** hote hain (jaise seasonal sales ya fluctuating traffic).
* Yeh automatically data ko mukhtalif tiers (Frequent Access, Infrequent Access, aur Archive Instant Access) ke darmiyan move karti hai bina kisi retrieval fee ke. Is se aapki cost optimize ho jati hai jab data access kam ho, aur jab sale aati hai toh data automatically frequent tier mein shift ho jata hai. Yeh standard S3 ki tarah **11 9s durability** aur **high availability** provide karta hai.



*Kyunki baqi options ghalat hain:*

* *S3 Express One Zone:* Yeh sirf **ek hi Availability Zone (Single AZ)** mein data store karta hai, isliye multi-AZ high availability ki requirement ko poora nahi karta.
* *S3 Standard-IA:* Yeh un files ke liye best hai jinki access frequency consistently low ho (kam az kam 30 din tak), lekin fluctuating/unpredictable access patterns ke liye Intelligent-Tiering behtar hai kyunki yeh khud-ba-khud adjust karta hai.
* *S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval:* Archive data ke liye hota hai jise instant access chahiye ho, lekin fluctuating retail transaction data ke liye jo achanak active ho jaye, Intelligent-Tiering zyada flexible aur standard option hai.
