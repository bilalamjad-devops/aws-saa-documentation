45. Question

A company requires corporate IT governance and cost oversight of all of its AWS resources across its divisions around the world. Their corporate divisions want to maintain administrative control of the discrete AWS resources they consume and ensure that those resources are separate from other divisions.

Which of the following options will support the autonomy of each corporate division while enabling the corporate IT to maintain governance and cost oversight? (Select TWO.)

- Use AWS Trusted Advisor and AWS Resource Groups Tag Editor
- Create separate Availability Zones for each division within the corporate IT AWS account. Improve communication between the two AZs using the AWS Global Accelerator.
- `Use AWS Consolidated Billing by creating AWS Organizations to link the divisions’ accounts to a parent corporate account.`
- `Enable IAM cross-account access for all corporate IT administrators in each child account.`
- Create separate VPCs for each division within the corporate IT AWS account. Launch an AWS Transit Gateway with equal-cost multipath routing (ECMP) and VPN tunnels for intra-VPC communication.

### 1. Sawal Kya Keh Raha Hai? (Question Breakdown) ❓

* **Scenario:** Ek company ke mukhtalif global divisions hain. Har division chahta hai ke uske paas apne resources par poora administrative control (autonomy) ho aur doosre division se separate rahein. Lekin **Corporate IT** yeh chahti hai ke unke paas poori company ki **governance (control)** aur **cost oversight (billing tracking)** ho.
* **Requirements:**
1. Divisions ko apas mein independent/autonomous hona chahiye.
2. Corporate IT ke paas central governance aur billing honi chahiye.
3. **Select TWO** options choose karne hain.



---

### 2. Sahi Jawabat Kya Keh Rahe Hain? (Correct Answers - Select TWO) 💡

1. **Use AWS Consolidated Billing by creating AWS Organizations to link the divisions’ accounts to a parent corporate account.**
* *Wazahat:* Multi-account strategy ke liye **AWS Organizations** best tareeqa hai. Har division ko apna alag AWS account diya jata hai jisse unhein mukammal administrative autonomy milti hai, aur Parent account ke zariye **Consolidated Billing** aur central policies lagai ja sakti hain.


2. **Enable IAM cross-account access for all corporate IT administrators in each child account.**
* *Wazahat:* Jab har division ka alag account hoga, toh corporate IT administrators ko un child accounts ko manage aur govern karne ke liye **Cross-account IAM roles/access** ki zaroorat hogi taake woh central level par control rakh sakein.



*Kyunki baqi options ghalat hain:*

* *Single account ke andar separate AZs ya separate VPCs banana:* Yeh multi-division governance ke liye theek nahi kyunki ek hi account mein billing, service limits, aur security boundaries share hoti hain, jo divisions ki strict separation requirement ke khilaf hai.

Aap ka yeh sawal bohot hi lajawab aur technical point par hai! 🧠

Jab hum **IAM Cross-Account Access** ki baat karte hain, toh woh **under-the-hood mechanism** hai (ke AWS permissions kaise cross account kaam karti hain). Lekin jab company bohot bari ho aur hazaron employees ya admins hon, toh har account ke liye alag IAM users banana ya manual cross-account roles switch karna mushkil ho jata hai.

Yahan entry hoti hai **AWS IAM Identity Center** (pehle iska naam **AWS SSO** tha) ki. Iska farq yeh hai:

---

### 1. IAM Cross-Account Access (The Engine ⚙️)

* Yeh aik permission model hai jo batata hai ke *Account A ka user Account B ko kaise access karega* (Assume Role ke zariye).
* Yeh backend par kaam karta hai, lekin isme har user ka apna alag IAM user banana par sakta hai agar central login na ho.

### 2. AWS IAM Identity Center (The Management Portal 🏢)

* **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Company ke saare employees/admins aik hi central portal par login karte hain (jaise corporate Google/Microsoft credentials ya Active Directory se).
* **Multi-Account Assignment:** Central portal se hi aap decide karte hain ke kis developer ko kis division ke kis AWS account mein **konsa role** (Admin, ReadOnly, etc.) milna chahiye.
* **Benefits:**
* Bar bar alag accounts mein passwords ya access keys manage nahi karni partiin.
* Ek jagah se ek click par saare accounts ki access di ya wapas li ja sakti hai (Centralized Governance).



---

### Summary Farq:

* **IAM Cross-Account Access** woh technology hai jo AWS accounts ko aapas mein connect karti hai.
* **IAM Identity Center** woh user-friendly dashboard hai jahan baith kar aap apne users ko manage karte hain aur woh usi cross-account mechanism ke zariye doosray accounts tak secure access pate hain.
