51. Question

A company needs to deploy at least two Amazon EC2 instances to support the normal workloads of its application and automatically scale up to six EC2 instances to handle the peak load. The architecture must be highly available and fault-tolerant as it is processing mission-critical workloads.

As a Solutions Architect, what should be done to meet this requirement?

- Create an Auto Scaling group of EC2 instances and set the minimum capacity to 4 and the maximum capacity to 6. Deploy 2 instances in Availability Zone A and another 2 instances in Availability Zone B.
- Create an Auto Scaling group of EC2 instances and set the minimum capacity to 2 and the maximum capacity to 6. Deploy 4 instances in Availability Zone A.
- `Create an Auto Scaling group of EC2 instances and set the minimum capacity to 2 and the maximum capacity to 6. Use 2 Availability Zones and deploy 1 instance for each AZ.`
- Create an Auto Scaling group of EC2 instances and set the minimum capacity to 2 and the maximum capacity to 4. Deploy 2 instances in Availability Zone A and 2 instances in Availability Zone B.

Boht zabardast point uthaya hai aap ne! Is concept ko Auto Scaling Group (ASG) ke working mechanism ke sath samjhein:

---

### Key Concept: Auto Scaling Instant Self-Healing 🛠️

Jab aap **Minimum Capacity = 2** rakhte hain aur **2 Availability Zones (AZ-A aur AZ-B)** select karte hain:

1. **Normal State:**
* **AZ-A:** 1 EC2 Instance
* **AZ-B:** 1 EC2 Instance
* Total = 2 Instances (Requirement Fulfilled)


2. **When AZ-A Fails (Datacenter Down):**
* AZ-A wala instance ek dam khatam ho jata hai.
* Total instances bachte hain = **1 (AZ-B mein)**.


3. **Auto Scaling Action (Within 1–2 Minutes):**
* AWS Auto Scaling Health Check detect kar leta hai ke minimum capacity 2 honi chahiye par abhi 1 bachi hai.
* Auto Scaling **fauran healthy AZ-B mein ek naya (2nd) EC2 instance launch kar deta hai**.
* System wapas 2 instances par aa jata hai!



---

### Standard AWS Exam Logic 📐

AWS exams mein jab question mein likha ho ke **"normal workload = 2 instances"** aur options mein minimum capacity ka pucha jaye:

* **Minimum Capacity = 2** hi right setting hoti hai, kyun ke normal routing 2 par chalti hai.
* Agar AZ fail ho bhi jaye, toh AWS Auto Scaling **Self-Healing** feature ke zariye target count (2) poora karne ke liye baaqi bache AZ mein naya instance khud spin-up kar deta hai.
