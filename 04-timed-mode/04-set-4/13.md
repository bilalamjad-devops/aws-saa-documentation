13. Question


Every week, an e-commerce company announces a sales promotion, causing its application hosted on an Auto Scaling group to experience intermittent downtime. Because of long initialization times, the application only becomes operational minutes before a new EC2 instance turns into RUNNING state. A solutions architect must devise a solution that launches capacity in advance based on a forecasted load in order to scale faster.

Which solution meets the requirements with the least amount of effort?

- Create a dynamic scaling policy based on the historical average CPU load of the application.
- `Configure the Auto Scaling group to use predictive scaling.`
- Use Amazon SageMaker Clarify to analyze and predict the workload pattern of the application. Create a scheduled scaling policy based on the prediction results.
- Create a Scheduled Amazon EventBridge (Amazon CloudWatch Events) Rule that runs a scaling job on a Lambda function every midnight.


### Question Kya Keh Raha Hai? ❓

* **Scenario:** Ek e-commerce company har hafte sales promotion karti hai, jiski wajah se Auto Scaling group par achanak boht zyada traffic aa jata hai.
* **Problem:** EC2 instances ko boot aur initialize hone mein waqt lagta hai (long initialization times), isliye jab tak naye instances operational hote hain, application down ho chuki hoti hai.
* **Requirement:** Ek aisi solution chahiye jo **forecasted load (pehle se andaza lagakar) ke mutabiq capacity ko advance mein launch karde** taake fast scaling ho sakay aur **kam se kam mehnat (least amount of effort)** lage.

---

### Correct Answer Kya Keh Raha Hai? 💡

* **Configure the Auto Scaling group to use predictive scaling.**
* **Wajah (Reason):** **Predictive Scaling** machine learning use kar ke historical traffic patterns ko analyze karta hai aur future load ko predict karta hai. Yeh automatically traffic spike hone se pehle hi instances ko advance mein launch kar deta hai, jis se initialization time ki waja se hone wali downtime khatam ho jati hai aur manual effort bhi nahi karni parti.

**Dynamic Scaling** is liye theek nahi hai kyunki yeh **reactive** hoti hai (yaani event hone ke *baad* action leti hai):

* **Kyun fail ho jati hai?** Dynamic scaling tab trigger hoti hai jab CPU load actual mein barh jata hai. Lekin is scenario mein problem yeh hai ke EC2 instances ko boot aur initialize hone mein waqt lagta hai. Jab tak CPU load barhega aur dynamic scaling naya instance launch karegi, tab tak der ho chuki hogi aur users ko downtime mil chuki hogi.
* **Predictive Scaling ka farq:** Yeh **proactive** hoti hai. Yeh pehle se machine learning ke zariye historical patterns analyze kar ke andaza laga leti hai ke kab traffic spike aane wala hai, aur traffic aane se **pehle hi** capacity advance mein launch kar deti hai. Is liye yeh long initialization times wale maslay ka behtareen hal hai.
