35. Question

A company has a High Performance Computing (HPC) cluster that is composed of EC2 Instances with Provisioned IOPS (io1) volume to process transaction-intensive, low-latency workloads. The Solutions Architect must maintain high IOPS while keeping the latency down by setting the optimal queue length for the volume. The size of each volume is 10 GiB.

Which of the following is the MOST suitable configuration that the Architect should set up?

- Set the IOPS to 400 then maintain a low queue length.
- Set the IOPS to 800 then maintain a low queue length.
- `Set the IOPS to 500 then maintain a low queue length.`
- Set the IOPS to 600 then maintain a high queue length.

### Question Kya Keh Raha Hai? ❓

* **Scenario:** Ek HPC (High Performance Computing) cluster hai jisme EC2 instances ke sath Provisioned IOPS (`io1`) volumes lage hue hain jo transaction-intensive aur low-latency workloads process karte hain.
* **Volume Size:** Har volume ka size **10 GiB** hai.
* **Requirement:** Solutions Architect ko high IOPS maintain karni hai aur latency ko low rakhna hai by setting the optimal queue length.

---

### Correct Answer Kya Keh Raha Hai? 💡

* **Set the IOPS to 500 then maintain a low queue length.**

---

### Wajah / Key Rule 🔍

* **50:1 Ratio Rule for `io1` volumes:** Amazon EBS ke `io1` volume type ke liye rule yeh hai ke **maximum ratio of provisioned IOPS to requested volume size (in GiB) 50:1** hota hai (yaani har 1 GiB par maximum 50 IOPS provision kiye ja sakte hain).
* Kyunke is question mein volume ka size sirf **10 GiB** hai, isliye maximum allowed IOPS honge:

$$\text{10 GiB} \times \text{50 IOPS/GiB} = \text{500 IOPS}$$


* Is wajah se IOPS ko 500 par set kiya ja sakta hai (800 isliye nahi ho sakta kyunki woh 10 GiB ke liye limit exceed kar jayega). Low queue length maintain karne se disk latency kam rehti hai.
