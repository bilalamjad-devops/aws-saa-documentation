8. Question
A company has a top priority requirement to monitor certain database metrics and send email notifications to the Operations team if any issues occur.

Which combination of AWS services can accomplish this requirement? (Select TWO.)

- `Amazon Simple Notification Service (SNS)`
- `Amazon CloudWatch`
- Amazon Simple Queue Service (SQS)
- Amazon Simple Email Service
- Amazon EC2 Instance with a running Berkeley Internet Name Domain (BIND) Server.


### Keywords Scan 🔍

1. **"monitor certain database metrics"**
* **Trigger:** AWS resources, databases, and metrics performance tracking -> **Amazon CloudWatch**.


2. **"send email notifications to the Operations team"**
* **Trigger:** Automated pub/sub event alerts and email notifications for operational metrics -> **Amazon Simple Notification Service (SNS)**.



---

### Correct Answers (Select TWO)

1. **Amazon CloudWatch**
2. **Amazon Simple Notification Service (SNS)**

---

### Question Aur Answer Ka Breakdown 💡

#### Question Kya Keh Raha Hai? ❓

* Ek company ko apne AWS Database ki metrics (jaise CPU utilization, Memory, ya Connections) par **nazar rakhni (monitor karna)** hai.
* Agar koi metric threshold cross ho jaye (jaise CPU 90% se zyada ho jaye), toh **Operations team ko instant alarm / email notification** chali jaye.
* Is kaam ke liye kon se 2 AWS services aapas mein mil kar kaam karti hain?

#### Answer Kya Keh Raha Hai? 💡

* **Amazon CloudWatch:** Yeh database ke sabhi metrics ko **monitor** karta hai aur ek **Alarm** create karta hai.
* **Amazon Simple Notification Service (SNS):** Jaise hi CloudWatch Alarm trigger hota hai, CloudWatch foran **SNS Topic** ko alert karta hai, aur SNS Operations team ki email addresses par automated **Email Notification** send kar deta hai.

---

### Aap Ka Sawaal: SES (Simple Email Service) Kyun Nahi? ❓

Aap ne pucha ke **"Why not Amazon SES?"** — Yeh AWS exam ka boht popular trick question hai! Farq samajhte hain:

1. **Amazon SNS (Pub/Sub & System Notifications):**
* **Purpose:** Yeh *System-to-Human* aur *System-to-System* **alerts/notifications** ke liye bana hai.
* **CloudWatch Integration:** CloudWatch Alarms direct **SNS** ke sath native integrators hain. CloudWatch alarm se direct SES ko trigger nahi kiya ja sakta (bina Lambda ke).
* **Use Case:** "CPU High ho gaya!", "Database crash hua!", "Disk space full hai!".


2. **Amazon SES (Simple Email Service):**
* **Purpose:** Yeh **Transactional and Marketing Bulk Emails** (Commercial applications) ke liye hota hai.
* **Use Case:** Jab aap Daraz/Amazon se kuch khareedte hain aur aap ko order receipt milti hai, ya password reset email aati hai, ya newsletter aati hai.
* **Operational Overhead:** SES ko monitoring alerts ke liye set up karne mein zyada customization aur code lagta hai, jabki SNS aur CloudWatch ka 1-click built-in connection hota hai.



---

### Key Summary Table ⚡

| Service | Main Purpose | Native CloudWatch Alarm Integration? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Amazon CloudWatch** | Resource Monitoring & Alarms | **Yes** (Generates metrics/alarms) |
| **Amazon SNS** | Push Notifications (Email/SMS/Slack) | **Yes** (Directly triggered by CloudWatch Alarms) |
| **Amazon SES** | High-volume Transactional & Marketing Emails | **No** (Needs custom code/Lambda to connect to CloudWatch) |
